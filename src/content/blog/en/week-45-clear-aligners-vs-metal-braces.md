# Clear Aligners vs. Traditional Metal Braces

Are crowded or misaligned teeth holding back your confidence, but the prospect of metallic brackets and wires makes you hesitant?

Breakthroughs in multilayer polymers and automated biomechanical staging have made **clear aligners** the gold standard for adult and teen orthodontic rehabilitation.

## Key Differences

- **True Discretion:** Transparent medical-grade polyurethane is imperceptible even at conversational distances.
- **Dietary Freedom:** Simply remove your aligners during meals—no forbidden foods, hard nuts, or sticky snacks restrictions.
- **Effortless Oral Hygiene:** Brush and floss normally without threading around brackets or specialized wire picks.
- **Zero Soft-Tissue Irritation:** Smooth contoured edges prevent cheek lacerations and painful mouth ulcers.
