# Laser Whitening and Enamel Integrity

A question patients frequently bring to our consultation rooms: *"Doctor, will professional laser whitening erode my enamel or trigger permanent sensitivity?"*

The definitive scientific consensus: **Under calibrated dental supervision, clinical cold-laser whitening causes zero structural harm or dissolution to tooth enamel.**

## The Underlying Mechanism

Professional whitening formulas utilize specialized medical hydrogen peroxide activated by cold diode wavelengths. Oxygen free radicals break apart entrenched organic chromophores within microscopic enamel pores. The hydroxyapatite crystal architecture itself remains completely sound and intact.

## Sensitivity Mitigation Protocols

Our clinic pairs every whitening cycle with **bioactive calcium phosphate and potassium nitrate remineralizing shields**, diminishing post-treatment thermal sensitivity by over 95%.
