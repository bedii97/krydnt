# Flapless Guided Implantology

Historically, dental implant surgery carried associations of large scalpels, extensive stitches, and days of facial swelling. In modern 2026 clinical implantology, **computer-guided flapless surgery** has revolutionized the entire experience.

## What is a 3D Surgical Guide?

By merging our patient's low-dose **3D CBCT bone scan** with a digital intraoral optical impression, we evaluate bone density, cortical thickness, and nerve paths with fractional millimeter precision.

The exact trajectory, angulation, and depth are predetermined virtually. A custom 3D stereolithographic surgical template is then manufactured.

## 5 Transformative Advantages:

1. **No Scalpels, No Sutures:** The implant is placed through a keyhole opening identical to the fixture's diameter.
2. **Minimal Discomfort & Bleeding:** Without periosteal flap reflection, post-op pain is drastically reduced.
3. **Virtually No Facial Swelling:** Minimal trauma eliminates bruising and prominent edema.
4. **Immediate Recovery:** International and local patients routinely resume work and travel the very next day.
5. **Immediate Same-Day Provisionals:** When bone stability meets protocol criteria, custom screw-retained provisional crowns are secured on the very same appointment.
